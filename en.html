Who I am

JAKHON.UZ is my personal development portfolio. I use it to distinguish working beta software from projects that are still being developed and ideas that are only in the planning stage.

I focus on useful and understandable digital tools: a person should be able to figure out what the software does, how to use it and what limitations still exist.

Software projects

J1Calc is an Android calculator; Local_DB Universal is a local database for records and documents; Visual Pack focuses on OCR, images and PDF files; DB AI Core explores local AI assistance for searching through supplied documents.

I am also developing Telegram bot concepts, including content collection and channel publication workflows. Other areas include smart home prototypes and future mobile applications.

How I approach development

I start by understanding the task, intended users and the available data. Next come the application structure, an initial prototype and testing with practical examples.

Beta software is not the same as a finished public release. The release dates shown on the site represent development targets and may change after testing.
